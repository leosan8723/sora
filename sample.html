How do I get started?

Just click "Say Hello" to book a free call. We’ll chat about your needs and find the right path for you.

Is my information private?

Absolutely. We use secure, encrypted platforms to ensure your sessions and data stay 100% confidential.

Can I choose my own therapist?

Yes. While we provide a recommendation based on your goals, the final choice is always yours.

Do you offer online sessions?

Yes, we offer flexible video sessions so you can get help from the comfort of your own home.

How long is each session?

Standard sessions typically last 45 to 50 minutes, depending on your personalized plan.

What if I’m not sure what I need?

That’s perfectly okay. Our initial consultation is designed to help you figure out exactly where to start.
